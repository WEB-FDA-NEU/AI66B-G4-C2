Step 1: Frame the topic

A good discussion title invites a real conversation — it's specific enough to answer, open enough to disagree about.

  • Make the claim or question clear up front
  • Say why it matters to you right now
  • Avoid bait-y phrasing and sweeping generalisations

Step 2: Share your own take

Discussions work best when the OP doesn't just ask — it starts. Share what you currently do, what you've tried, or what changed your mind, and others will respond in kind.

Step 3: Invite disagreement

Good discussions welcome pushback. Instead of "am I right?", try "here's why I think X — what am I missing?". You'll get better replies.
